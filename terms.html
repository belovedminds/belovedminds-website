The service

Beloved Minds provides daily AI-powered wellness phone calls, wellness and engagement insights, and family notifications to memory care and assisted living communities under a facility-level agreement.

Not a medical or emergency service

Beloved Minds provides daily wellness connection. It is not a medical device, medical service, or emergency response service, and it does not replace clinical care, staff supervision, or emergency systems. In an emergency, always call 911.

Facility & family responsibilities
