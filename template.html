Why Beloved Minds

Every day in a memory care facility across America, a resident wakes up and waits — for someone to sit with them, ask about their life, and listen. That conversation may never come. Not because staff don't care, but because the math of caregiving makes it structurally impossible. Beloved Minds was built to change that math: a voice designed to heal as much as to speak, intelligence that watches for what words alone cannot say, and a daily presence that never forgets. We show up for the people everyone else couldn't get to — every single day.

The problem we solve

Bringing daily connection where it's needed most

Even the best care teams can't give every resident one-to-one time every single day. Families want to feel closer and more reassured, and the small day-to-day changes that matter deserve to be noticed early. Beloved Minds is built to help with all three.

🤍

Isolation accelerates decline

Research consistently links chronic loneliness to faster cognitive decline — affecting memory, mood, and behavioral regulation in people living with dementia.

⏱️

Attention doesn't scale

Even the most devoted staff cannot give every resident meaningful one-to-one time, every day. The need is structural, not a failing of care.

📉

Change goes unseen

Subtle shifts in mood and behavior are early signals — but without daily touchpoints they surface late, once a situation has already escalated.

The solution

A warm, familiar voice — every single day

The resident's phone simply rings, and a voice that knows their name, their stories, and their family is there for a personalized daily conversation. Every call is both a meaningful connection and a quiet daily observation.

📞

Calls reach the resident on familiar phone hardware — no new device to learn, no app, no tablet to lose or break.

💬

A voice that remembers

🛡️

A quiet daily observation

Each conversation helps surface changes in mood and engagement, and alerts staff and family promptly if a resident seems in distress.

What makes us different

It isn't only what the voice says. It's how it makes you feel.

Every other approach focuses on the words. Beloved Minds focuses on the voice itself — its warmth, tone, and pacing, purposefully designed to calm, reassure, and bring comfort to people living with dementia and Alzheimer's.

That is the heart of our platform: connection delivered through a voice engineered to feel safe and familiar.

The three that define us

🎚️

Cognitive Engagement Level

Every resident is set to one of five engagement levels. At Level 1, someone still holding a conversation gets open-ended questions, and Bella follows wherever they go. At Level 5 — someone who may say very little all day — she carries nearly the whole conversation, weaving their own family memories into one continuous story. They may not be able to say anything back. But the memory is still theirs, and hearing it can still bring them joy.

🧭

Confusion Response Mode

Families choose how Bella responds when a resident is confused: agree, gently redirect, or offer a gentle correction. This is the single most consequential setting in memory care. Families make the call once, and it is honored on every call, every day — no resident is ever argued with or made to feel small.

📖

Story Cards

Not every call produces one. But when a resident shares something that matters — a memory, a moment, something they'd want remembered — it becomes a formatted keepsake families can keep. The stories they tell, captured and preserved, so what they carry is never lost.

Mission & vision

Why we exist

Our Mission

To change what dementia is allowed to take.

It will take memory. We understand that. But it does not have to take a person's place in the world, their sense of being known, or the record of who they were. Those are losses the world has simply come to expect — and we don't.

Every day, for as long as we're needed.

Our Vision

A world where no one with dementia is left alone with what they can no longer hold. Where the details fade and someone is there to fill them in — to tell the story back, to say the names, to remember out loud what a person can no longer remember for themselves.

A world where a life keeps growing even as memory recedes. Where the stories that were never told are captured before they're gone, and a family who has been grieving someone still living is given something to hold when the time comes — not a record of decline, but a portrait of a whole person.

And a world where the people who do this work every day feel it. Where a facility isn't carrying one more thing, but is prouder of the care they give because of what we bring to it.

Our values

Facilities are not our customers. Families are not our users. They are our partners — and we are in this together for the long term. We measure our success by the depth of the relationships we build, not the number of contracts we sign.
