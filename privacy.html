Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy is being finalized. If you have questions about how we handle your information in the meantime, contact us at Rebeccamccallum@belovedminds.care.

Who we are

Beloved Minds LLC ("Beloved Minds," "we," "us") is an Idaho-based company providing daily AI-powered wellness phone calls to residents of memory care and assisted living communities, in partnership with those facilities and residents' families.

Information we collect

  • Enrollment information provided by the facility or family — such as a resident's name, phone number, and the personal details (family names, memories, preferences) used to personalize calls.
  • Call and wellness information — conversation content, engagement and mood signals, and wellness flags generated from calls.
  • Contact information you submit through our website forms or by email.

How we use it

  • To deliver and personalize daily wellness calls.
  • To surface wellness, mood, and engagement insights to authorized facility staff and family members.
  • To alert staff and family promptly if a resident appears to be in distress.
  • To operate, secure, and improve our service.

We do not sell personal information.

Security

Resident conversations and wellness data are encrypted, access-controlled, and handled to healthcare privacy standards. Beloved Minds is built to be HIPAA-compliant.

Your choices

Families and facilities may review, update, or request deletion of a resident's information, or unenroll a resident at any time, by contacting us.
