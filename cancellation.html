Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy is being finalized. Specific cancellation terms are set out in each facility's agreement. Questions: Rebeccamccallum@belovedminds.care.

Pilot programs

Pilot participation carries no long-term commitment — pilot terms are flexible, and a facility may end a pilot as described in its pilot agreement.

Facility agreements

Ongoing facility subscriptions may be cancelled per the notice period in the facility's agreement. We'll confirm the effective end date in writing and wind down calls gracefully so residents and families aren't surprised.

Individual residents

A family or facility may unenroll an individual resident at any time, effective the next calendar day, by contacting us or using the staff dashboard.

Your data after cancellation

After cancellation, resident information and call data are handled per our Privacy Policy — families and facilities may request export or deletion.
